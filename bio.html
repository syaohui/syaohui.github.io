Bio

Biography

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Applied Mathematics and Statistics at Stony Brook University, where I began graduate study in 2022 and advanced to candidacy in Fall 2025. My dissertation, Uncovering Causal Mechanisms of Aerosol-Cloud-Turbulence Interactions Using Physics- and Data-Driven Methods, is expected in May 2027.

From 2024 to 2026 I was a research assistant in the Environmental Science and Technologies Department at Brookhaven National Laboratory, working with Yangang Liu, Robert McGraw, and Tao Zhang on turbulence-microphysics interactions in clouds. That work, supported by the DOE Atmospheric System Research Program and a Brookhaven LDRD project, led to a first-author paper in Geophysical Research Letters on stochastic condensation with information flow.

Before Stony Brook I received an M.S. in Finance from the University of Rochester in 2022, and I hold the Certificate in Quantitative Finance. In Fall 2026 I am a teaching assistant for AMS 595, Fundamentals of Computing.
